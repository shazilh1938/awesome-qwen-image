## 🛠️ Troubleshooting Common Issues

### "I get an error saying something about missing files"
Don't panic! This usually happens when the initial download didn't complete fully. Simply close the app, delete the downloaded file, and download again from our link. Make sure your internet doesn't disconnect during download.

### "The application opens but images are generating slowly"
This is normal on computers without a high-end graphics card. Try reducing the image size in settings (e.g., from 1024x1024 to 512x512). You'll still get great results, just faster!

### "I see a message about 'CUDA' or 'GPU drivers'"
This means you need to update your graphics card drivers. Visit your GPU manufacturer's website (NVIDIA, AMD, or Intel) and download the latest driver for your specific card model. It takes about 5 minutes and fixes most issues.

### "The interface looks different from screenshots"
No problem - we update frequently with improvements. As long as your version is the latest (check via the app's "Check for Updates" feature), you're good to go. The functionality will be the same even if the layout varies slightly.
